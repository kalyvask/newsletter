# Voice and Style

Copy this file to `voice.md` and customize it. `voice.md` is gitignored, so your
personal voice notes stay local.

The text below is injected into the system prompts that draft the newsletter.
Anything here tells Claude *who the briefing is for* and *how it should sound*.
Keep it short — 3–8 lines is plenty.

---

## Audience

The reader is a busy operator who tracks AI for work — describe yourself or
your audience here (role, industry, what decisions you make from this briefing).

## Tone

Dense, opinionated, zero filler. Every sentence should carry a fact, a name,
or a clear take. Prefer present tense. Avoid generic statements like
"AI continues to evolve."

## What to emphasize

List 2–4 themes or angles you care about most — e.g. enterprise adoption,
open-source momentum, eval/observability, agent reliability. Claude will
weight coverage and synthesis toward these.

## What to skip

List anything that should be deprioritized — e.g. crypto crossover, generic
"AI is the future" punditry, vendor press releases without substance.
